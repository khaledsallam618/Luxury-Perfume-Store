Shipping & Return Policy

Clear, transparent, and fair policies for an exceptional shopping experience.

1. Domestic Shipping & Delivery

We work with trusted courier services to guarantee safe and expedited delivery right to your door:

  • Standard Delivery: Delivered within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Order Tracking: You will receive notifications regarding your package status once dispatched.
  • Careful Packaging: All bottles are securely cushioned to prevent any transit damage.

2. 14-Day Return Window

We accept return requests within 14 days of receiving your parcel under the following conditions:

  • The perfume bottle must remain unopened, unused, and sealed in its original manufacturer cellophane and packaging.
  • Proof of purchase or order confirmation is required.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your package arrives damaged or you received the incorrect fragrance, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will immediately replace the item or issue a full refund at no additional cost.

4. Refund Process

Once your returned item is received and inspected, your refund will be processed back to your original payment method (e.g., PayPal account) within 3 to 7 business days.

5. Starting a Return

To initiate a return or exchange, please reach out to our team directly via our Contact Form with your full name and order details.
