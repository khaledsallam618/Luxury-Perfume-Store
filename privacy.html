Last Updated: September 2026

When you visit Luxury Perfume or place an order, we may collect the following details:

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to:

3. Payment Security & Third Parties

All online financial transactions are processed securely through certified payment gateways such as PayPal. Luxury Perfume does not sell, lease, or distribute your personal data to external advertisers or unauthorized third parties.

Our website utilizes local storage and cookies to maintain your shopping cart items across sessions and preserve your preferences seamlessly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data, feel free to reach out through our Contact Us page.
